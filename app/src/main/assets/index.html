Events

    Trouble codes

    Connect, then tap Read codes. Stored codes are the ones that turned on the check-engine light; pending codes are problems seen once that haven't confirmed yet.

    Truck info

    Connect to see the protocol, supported sensors and battery voltage.

    Vehicle

    Gear ratios, axle ratio and tire size (below) are saved separately for each vehicle. When the adapter reports a VIN, DriveLog switches to the matching vehicle by itself and tags each drive with it.

    Adapter

    Pair the adapter once in your phone's Bluetooth settings (PIN is usually 1234 or 0000), then pick it here. Plug it in and turn the ignition on before connecting.
    ProtocolYour 2005 Colorado uses J1850 VPW. Auto also works but the first connect is slower.

    Automatic

    Connect automaticallyKeeps looking for your adapter and connects as soon as the truck powers it (in the background too, if that is on below).
    Keep logging in the backgroundLets DriveLog connect and record with the app closed and the screen off (an ongoing notification shows while it runs). Turn off to log only while DriveLog is open.
    Background notificationAndroid requires one while logging runs in the background. Minimal removes the status-bar icon and hides it on the lock screen. "Notification settings" opens Android's page where you can silence or hide it entirely (logging keeps running).
    Record automaticallyStarts a recording when the engine is running and saves the drive after the engine has been off for the delay below. Stopping by hand pauses it until the engine has been turned off.
    Engine-off delay (seconds)How long RPM must read zero before the drive is saved.
    DriveLog has to stay open (screen on) to log. Phone in a mount with the screen on works best.

    Sensors to log

    Fast sensors are read every cycle; Normal ones take turns (each about every few seconds). Fewer fast sensors = more readings per second. Greyed out = the truck doesn't report it (shown after connecting).

    Gear calculation

    The truck doesn't report the gear, so DriveLog works it out from RPM ÷ speed. Easiest: record a normal drive, open it under Drives and tap Learn gears from this drive.
    Gear ratios4L60-E automatic: 3.06, 1.63, 1.00, 0.70

    Tire diameter (inches)
    RPM per mph in 1:1 gearCalculated from the two above, or learned from a drive.

    Fuel economy (estimated)

    The truck reports air flow, not fuel. DriveLog assumes gasoline burned at the ideal 14.7:1 mixture and works out MPG from the MAF sensor (needs MAF in the sensor list). Hold the Fuel economy tile on Live to read more.
    Adjust for fuel trimsThe computer adds or removes fuel (short + long term trim) to hold the mixture. On: the estimate follows it. Off: pure air flow ÷ 14.7.
    Live average overHow far back the Live tile averages. Tapping the tile cycles through these.
